# Tarek Omar Designs — TOD Studio Promo
### Voiceover script · ~43 seconds · social / WhatsApp cut

**Tone:** premium, confident, elegant. Braggy through what it *shows*, not what it claims.
**Read:** unhurried, ~145 wpm. Let the silences sit — the pauses are what make it feel expensive.
**VO word count:** 102

---

## Notes

**On the 15-second cut** (WhatsApp / Stories): keep scenes 1, 4 and 5 only. The drawing, the snap, the walk-in. Open on the dimension line, close on the wordmark. Everything else is expendable — the snap and the walk-in are the whole pitch.

**On "easier, faster, more visual":** this lands in scene 6, and it's phrased as removals rather than claims — *no waiting on drawings, no guesswork*. Saying "faster and easier" outright is the single quickest way to sound like every other studio's ad. Naming what disappears says the same thing and sounds like confidence instead of marketing.

**On the brag:** the competitive edge is carried by scenes 1–2 as a hard cut (mood board / the room) and then demonstrated by 4 and 5. Resist adding lines like "ahead of traditional interior design" — the film already outclasses the competition by showing a client walking through their own kitchen. Asserting it as well is what would tip into cheesy.

**On the tagline:** *Buy Your Happiness* lands on screen, not in the read. Spoken, it competes with the closing line; on screen it sits under the wordmark and does its job quietly.

**On honesty:** the site is a preview build. Scene 3 says "is now online", which is true of the preview and doesn't overclaim a full launch. If you'd rather be explicit, swap in: *"Tarek Omar Designs is now online — first look."*
