# Tarek Omar Designs — TOD Studio Promo
### Voiceover script · ~42 seconds · social / WhatsApp cut

**Tone:** premium, confident, elegant. Braggy through what it *shows*, not what it claims.
**Read:** unhurried, ~145 wpm. Let the silences sit — the pauses are what make it feel expensive.
**VO word count:** 97

---

### 1 · 0:00–0:05 — The drawing
**On screen:** Paper. A brass dimension line draws itself left to right. The figure settles: **3,600 mm**. Near-silence, one dry tick.

> **VO:** Most interior studios hand you a mood board.

---

### 2 · 0:05–0:10 — It stands up
**On screen:** The flat plan lifts and extrudes into a full room. Paper darkens to near-black.

> **VO:** We hand you the room.

---

### 3 · 0:10–0:15 — The site
**On screen:** Quick, confident scroll through the Tarek Omar Designs preview site. Hold on the **Design Your Space** button.

> **VO:** Tarek Omar Designs is now online. And this is the part everyone stops on.

---

### 4 · 0:15–0:28 — TOD Studio
**On screen:** The Studio. Cursor clicks a catalogue row — a cabinet flies in and **snaps flush** to the wall. A second latches beside it. A finish swaps across every door at once. Plan view snaps to 3D.

> **VO:** TOD Studio. Draw your space. Drop in real kitchen modules — they snap flush, to the millimetre. Change a finish, and the whole room changes with it. Flip from plan to 3D…

**Direction:** The two snaps carry their own sound. Don't music over them — they're the moment the client believes it.

---

### 5 · 0:28–0:34 — Inside
**On screen:** Chrome falls away. Camera drops to eye height and walks forward into the finished kitchen.

> **VO:** …then walk inside it. Before a single thing is built.

---

### 6 · 0:34–0:39 — Save & quote
**On screen:** Project saves. Quote request sends. Clean, fast, no clutter.

> **VO:** Save it. Send it. Your quote comes back on the exact design you just made.

---

### 7 · 0:39–0:45 — Close
**On screen:** Wordmark **TAREK OMAR DESIGNS**, letterspaced. Beneath it, small: *Buy Your Happiness*. Hold in near-silence.

> **VO:** Tarek Omar Designs. Design your space — before it exists.

---

## Notes

**On the 15-second cut** (WhatsApp / Stories): keep scenes 1, 4 and 5 only. The drawing, the snap, the walk-in. Open on the dimension line, close on the wordmark. Everything else is expendable — the snap and the walk-in are the whole pitch.

**On the brag:** the confidence should come from scene 4 and 5 doing something a mood board can't. Resist adding claim lines like "revolutionising interior design" — the film already outclasses the competition by showing a client walking through their own kitchen. Saying it as well is what would make it cheesy.

**On the tagline:** *Buy Your Happiness* lands on screen, not in the read. Spoken, it competes with the closing line; on screen it sits under the wordmark and does its job quietly.

**On honesty:** the site is a preview build. Scene 3 says "is now online", which is true of the preview and doesn't overclaim a full launch. If you'd rather be explicit, swap in: *"Tarek Omar Designs is now online — first look."*
